# Making a Good Presentation

Presentations are an essential part of academic and professional life. Here are some tips to help you create a compelling presentation:

## Structuring Your Presentation

Follow APSET guidelines for slide design:

- **A**: Area, Define the area of your work
- **P**: Problem, Define the problem you are solving
- **S**: Solution, Your approach to solving the problem
- **E**: Evaluation, Your method of evaluation and results
- **T**: Takeaway, The main point you want your audience to remember

## Design Principles

- Colourful slides, use a consistent color scheme
- Using a white background with dark text is usually best for visibility
- 20 or 24 font size for the main text
- Slides should not be crowded.
- Make big and clear block diagrams
- A maximum of 4-5 bullets and 4-5 words per bullet

## Content Guidelines

- Keep text concise and to the point.
- Use bullet points to organize information.
- Keep material in an appendix.
- You need not present references slide unless asked.
- If you are presenting more than one problem, motivate each problem
  - Why this problem specifically?
  - What have you done to solve it?
  - What are your results?
